Our Story

The People Behind AURORA

We are a team of designers, engineers, and strategists united by a single belief: great digital work changes the world.

Founded in 2012

Born From a Single Question

AURORA began when our founders asked: "Why can't digital work be beautiful and effective at the same time?" That question has guided every project since.

Over twelve years we've grown from a two-person studio in San Francisco to a 40-strong international team serving clients across five continents. Yet our approach remains unchanged — close collaboration, honest communication, and relentless craft.
